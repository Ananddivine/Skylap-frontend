Laptop repair cost in Bangalore

Search this question and you get a dozen national price tables that disagree with each other by two or three times. They disagree because the price is set by your exact model and the exact fault, not by the words "screen" or "motherboard". This guide explains what actually moves each price so you can judge any quote you are given, including ours.

Updated 2026-09-10Bengaluru market, independent workshops and official channels compared

Quick answer

Two rules cover most of it. Parts jobs (screen, battery, keyboard, hinge, SSD, RAM, fan) are priced by the part your model takes, plus fitting: a range can be given honestly. Fault jobs (motherboard, liquid damage, GPU, charging circuit, data recovery) are priced by what failed, so any number quoted before a diagnosis is a guess. That is why every Bengaluru workshop, including us, quotes those after a free check.

Why two shops quote you differently for the "same" repair

Three legitimate reasons, and one that is not. Legitimate: the part grade (an original-grade panel and a budget compatible panel are different products); what the price includes (part only, part plus fitting, plus GST, plus warranty); and what they found (a screen job that also needs a cable is not the same job). Not legitimate: a low headline price that grows once the laptop is opened. Ask any workshop for one written price covering parts and labour before work starts, and ask what the warranty is. We put both on the quote.

What "free diagnosis" should mean

At a workshop it should mean you can bring the laptop in, have the fault identified and be told the price, at no cost, with no obligation to proceed. Doorstep and platform services in Bengaluru commonly charge a visit or inspection fee, often adjusted against the repair if you go ahead. Ask which applies before booking. At our Whitefield bench the check is free; we tell you what we found and what it costs, and you decide.

Where official service centres fit

For a laptop still in warranty, the authorised centre is the right first stop; use it. Out of warranty, the difference is structural rather than a matter of trust: authorised centres replace assemblies (a board, a display assembly) because that is their process, while an independent workshop can repair the component that failed. On board faults that difference is large. On simple parts jobs it is smaller, and sometimes the official part is worth the premium.

  • In warranty: authorised centre, always.
  • Out of warranty, parts job: compare the part grade, not just the price.
  • Out of warranty, board fault: a component-level repair is usually a fraction of a board replacement.
  • Data at risk: ask who touches the drive and what the policy is.

Questions worth asking before you hand over a laptop

  • Is the diagnosis free, and is there any charge if I decline the repair?
  • Will I get one written price covering parts, labour and GST before work starts?
  • Which part grade are you fitting, and what warranty comes with the part and the labour?
  • What happens to my data, and can you back it up first?
  • If the repair fails, what do I pay?
  • How will you tell me the turnaround, and what happens if a part has to be ordered?
