Buying a refurbished laptop: what to check

A well-chosen refurbished laptop is excellent value; a badly chosen one becomes a repair bill. The difference is fifteen minutes of checking, and every item below can be checked in front of the seller.

Updated 2026-09-10

Quick answer

Check four things in this order: battery health (the Windows battery report), drive health and type, the screen under a white and a black background, and the hinges. Then run something demanding for ten minutes and feel the exhaust. Ask for the invoice, the warranty in writing and the exact model number.

The checklist

  • Battery report: run powercfg /batteryreport and compare design capacity with full-charge capacity. A worn pack is a cost you are inheriting.
  • Drive: is it an SSD, and what is its health and hours? A hard disk in a refurbished machine means you will be upgrading it.
  • Screen: full-white and full-black images reveal dead pixels, patches, backlight bleed and pressure marks.
  • Hinges and body: open and close a few times; look for cracks at the corners. Hinge damage is common in used machines.
  • Keyboard: type every key, including the number row and arrows.
  • Ports: test every USB port, HDMI, audio and the card reader. Several dead ports can mean board damage.
  • Thermals: run a demanding task for ten minutes; a machine that gets very hot and loud needs a thermal service, which is cheap but worth knowing about.
  • Wi-Fi and camera: connect, make a call.
  • Model number and paperwork: the exact model, an invoice, and the warranty terms in writing.

What "refurbished" should mean

The word is unregulated, so ask what was actually done:

  • Was the battery tested or replaced, and what does the report show now?
  • Was the drive replaced, and is it new?
  • Was the cooling cleaned and re-pasted?
  • What was replaced, and what was only cleaned?
  • What warranty covers the machine, and who honours it?

Questions people ask

Before you decide

Are refurbished laptops good?

A properly refurbished machine with a healthy battery, an SSD and a warranty is very good value. The risk is in machines sold as refurbished with nothing done to them, which the checklist exposes.

Where can I buy a refurbished laptop in Bangalore?

Specialist sellers, marketplaces, and workshops like ours. What matters more than the channel is whether you can inspect the machine and get a warranty in writing.

Are refurbished laptops reliable?

As reliable as what was done to them. A laptop with a new battery, a new SSD and a serviced cooling system is a different proposition from a cleaned-up old machine.
