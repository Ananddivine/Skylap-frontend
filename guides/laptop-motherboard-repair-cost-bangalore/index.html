What decides a motherboard repair price

No workshop in Bengaluru publishes a fixed chip-level price, and the honest reason is that the price is the fault. A charging IC, a shorted capacitor, a corroded board after a spill and a failed GPU are four different jobs with four different bills.

Quick answer

Component-level repair prices track the fault: input and charging circuits at the lower end, power-section shorts in the middle, liquid damage and GPU rework at the top. A replacement board is usually several times a component repair, because you are buying the processor too. Free diagnosis first is not a sales tactic here; it is the only way to price it.

Component-level board repair, by faultQuote after free diagnosis

Charging IC, power section or short, BIOS, liquid damage, GPU or PCH rework. One fault or several.

Board replacement (when repair is not sensible)Quote after free diagnosis

New or refurbished board, gaming or premium, part availability, BIOS and serial programming.

Data recoveryQuote after free diagnosis

Logical, failing drive, mechanical or SSD controller failure; encryption; clean-room referral.

The fault types, cheapest to dearest

  • BIOS corruption: reprogram the chip. The board is otherwise fine.
  • Charging or power IC: replace one component. The most common "dead laptop" fault.
  • Short circuit in the power section: find the shorted part among many; more diagnosis time.
  • Liquid damage: clean the board, then repair everything corrosion reached. Time and uncertainty both rise with delay.
  • GPU or PCH rework: reball or replace a large chip; needs donor availability and carries real risk. Quoted with the odds.

Short circuit on the board: what it means for the price

A short circuit is a component that has failed by connecting power straight to ground. The laptop protects itself by refusing to start, so the symptoms look like a dead laptop:

  • No power at all, or the power light flashes once and goes out.
  • The charger light dims or goes off when it is plugged into the laptop.
  • One spot on the underside gets warm while the laptop is plugged in but off.

What sets the price is where the short is. A shorted capacitor on the charger input line is quick to find and cheap to replace. A short on the processor or graphics power rail sits among dozens of parts and takes longer to locate, and if the short is inside the processor or graphics chip itself, the honest answer may be a replacement board. That is why a short is diagnosed first and priced second.

Until then, stop plugging it in. Repeated attempts push current through the fault and can damage the parts around it.

Repair or replace the board?

A board replacement includes the processor and, on many laptops, the graphics: that is why it costs several times a component repair, and why authorised centres quote so much for a "dead motherboard". Replacement makes sense when the processor or chipset itself has failed, or when corrosion has spread too far. Otherwise repairing the board you own is both cheaper and better for your data, because the same storage and the same machine stay together.

What data recovery depends on

Recovery is priced by failure type, not by gigabytes:

  • Laptop dead, drive healthy: the simplest case; often part of the repair.
  • Logical loss (deleted, formatted, corrupt file system) on a healthy drive: software recovery, good odds.
  • Failing drive with bad sectors: imaged sector by sector first, then recovered from the image.
  • Mechanical failure or a dead SSD controller: clean-room or chip-level work, referred to a lab that quotes separately.

Questions people ask

Before you decide

How much is motherboard repair in Bangalore?

It is quoted after a free diagnosis, because the failed component sets the price. Anyone quoting a firm number before seeing the board is guessing, and the guess usually rises later.

Is motherboard repair worth it?

For a single component failure on a laptop that is otherwise good, yes, comfortably. For a corroded board on an old, slow machine, usually not, and we will say so.

How much does a motherboard replacement cost?

Much more than a component repair, because the board carries the processor. We quote a replacement only when repair is not the honest answer.

What is the cost of data recovery in India?

Workshop-level recovery from a healthy drive is modest; clean-room recovery from a mechanically failed drive is a lab service with lab pricing. We tell you which category you are in before anything is charged.
