Rest of Bengaluru

Marathahalli, Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR, Bellandur, Electronic City, Banashankari and anywhere else in the city: we arrange a courier both ways and tell you the cost up front before anything moves. The repair itself happens at the same Whitefield bench by the same technician. For a straightforward parts job this is often not worth your money compared with a good local shop, and we will say so; for chip-level board work, where component-level repair is the difference between a repair and a new board, it usually is.

To be explicit: there is no SkyLap branch in any of those areas. Listings that suggest otherwise are wrong, and creating fake location pages to rank for them is not something we do.

What happens to your laptop

  • You get a record of the laptop and its condition at collection, with your name and number.
  • Free diagnosis at the bench, then the fault and one written price by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Nothing is opened further or ordered until you approve.
  • Return to the same address, free within the areas above.

When it is faster to come in

If you are nearby and the fault is simple, walking in is quicker: many small fixes are done while you wait. The workshop is on the first floor opposite Dress Circle Mall.

Questions people ask

Before you decide

Is pickup and drop really free?

Yes, within the areas listed above. Elsewhere in Bengaluru we arrange a courier and tell you the cost before collecting.

Do you have a branch in Marathahalli or Koramangala?

No. One workshop, in Whitefield. We collect from other areas by courier, and we say so rather than implying branches we do not have.

How do I arrange a pickup?

Call +91 96061 20007 or send a WhatsApp message with your area and the fault. A photo helps.

Is my laptop insured during transport?

Ask us when you book; we will tell you exactly what applies rather than making a claim on a web page.

Not sure which one you have?

Describe it in a sentence or send a photo. We tell you what it is before you spend anything.
