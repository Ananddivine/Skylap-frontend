Laptop repair near ITPL

If you work in one of the ITPL or EPIP Zone parks, you do not need to lose an evening to a laptop repair. We collect from the gate or the reception, repair at our Whitefield bench a few minutes away, and bring it back.

The workshop is on Whitefield Main Road, minutes from the ITPL gates, which is why office collection works here and why a laptop dropped in the morning is often looked at the same day. Tell us the fault on WhatsApp with a photo, and we tell you what it is likely to be before we come.

How office collection works

  • Tell us where and when. Gate, reception, or a café at lunchtime; we fit around your working day.
  • We collect and give you a record of the laptop and its condition, with your name and number.
  • Free diagnosis at the bench, then a call or WhatsApp message with the fault and one written price.
  • You approve, and we tell you the expected ready time.
  • We deliver it back to the same place, free, within our pickup radius.

What we see most from tech-park laptops

  • Thermal throttling. Machines that live in bags and on desks with the vents blocked. A clean and re-paste restores the speed. Thermal service.
  • Battery wear. Laptops kept on charge all day age their packs quickly. Battery replacement.
  • Charging faults. Daily plugging and unplugging wears the jack; the charging circuit follows. Charging repair.
  • Hinge and screen damage. Bag pressure and one-corner opening. Hinge repair.
  • Slow machines. Personal laptops still on hard disks. SSD upgrade.

Company laptops

If the laptop belongs to your employer, check with your IT team before handing it to any third party; many have their own support contract and asset rules. We are happy to give you a written diagnosis to pass on.
