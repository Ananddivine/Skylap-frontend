Quick answer

A cracked or shattered screen means a new panel: glass-only repair does not exist for laptop LCDs. If the screen is black, dim, flickering or shows lines but is not cracked, the fault may be the display cable, the hinge pulling on it, or the board, and replacing the panel would waste your money. We test with an external monitor and a known-good cable before we quote.

Symptoms this covers

  • Cracked or shattered glass, ink-like black patches, or a spider-web pattern
  • "Screen stopped working" while the laptop is clearly on
  • Vertical or horizontal lines, colour tints, or half the screen dim
  • Flicker that changes when you move the lid
  • Very dim picture that you can only just see against a light
  • Touch not responding on a touchscreen model

Likely causes, in plain words

CommonPhysical damage to the panel
Dropped, closed on an earring or pen, pressure in a bag. Only a new panel fixes this.
SometimesDisplay cable or connector
The eDP cable runs through the hinge and wears. Flicker on lid movement is the classic sign. Far cheaper than a panel. Lines and flicker explained
SometimesHinge damage stressing the cable
A hinge broken on one side twists the lid and the cable inside it. Hinge repair
SometimesBacklight or board fault
Picture is there but too dim to see, or no picture with the laptop running: the backlight circuit or the board, not the glass. Turns on but no display
RareGraphics chip
Artefacts on both the laptop screen and an external monitor point at the GPU, a board-level job. Chip-level repair

How we diagnose it

  1. We connect an external monitor. If the picture is fine there, the problem is in the lid, not the board.
  2. We inspect the hinge and cable route with the bezel off and test with a known-good cable.
  3. We identify the exact panel: size, resolution, refresh rate, connector pin count, touch or not, bracket type. Two laptops of the same model can use different panels.
  4. You get one written price for the correct part and the fitting, before anything is ordered.

Repair options

Panel replacement

A new panel matched to your model. We tell you whether it is an original-grade or compatible panel and what that means for brightness and colour, so the choice is yours.

Cable, connector or hinge repair

If the panel is fine, we fix what is actually broken. This is the outcome nobody else mentions because it earns less.

Upgrade while it is open

On many models a 60 Hz TN panel can be replaced by an IPS or higher-refresh panel of the same size and connector. We say when that is possible and when it is not.

What it costs

What decides the price

  • Size: 11.6, 13.3, 14, 15.6, 16 or 17.3 inches
  • Resolution: HD (1366×768) vs Full HD vs QHD or 4K
  • Panel type: TN vs IPS vs OLED
  • Refresh rate: 60 Hz vs 120/144/165/240 Hz on gaming models
  • Touch or bonded touch assembly, which is a different part altogether
  • Narrow-bezel assemblies that need lid or hinge work to fit
  • Part grade: original-grade or compatible, and availability for older models
  • Whether the cable or hinge also needs replacing

We quote after a free diagnosis of your specific model, in writing, before any work starts. There is no charge if you decide not to go ahead.

Repair, or replace the laptop?

Repair makes sense when

  • The laptop is under about five years old and otherwise healthy
  • Only the panel is damaged and the panel is available
  • You want to keep your data and setup untouched

Replacement makes sense when

  • The panel price approaches the value of the machine and the battery and board are also tired
  • It is a discontinued model with no panel available in India
  • You were already planning an upgrade; we can move your data to the new one

Questions people ask

Before you decide

My screen is cracked. Can it be repaired instead of replaced?

No. Laptop LCD and OLED panels are sealed units; a crack anywhere means the panel is replaced. What we do check is whether anything else was damaged in the same knock, so you pay for one repair, not two.

The screen stopped working but the laptop is on. Is it the panel or the cable?

Often the cable or the board. Connect an external monitor: if that works, the board is fine and the fault is in the lid. Flicker when you move the lid points strongly at the cable. We test both before quoting.

Original or compatible panel: which do you fit?

Both exist and we tell you which is going in and the price difference. Original-grade panels match the brightness and colour of the factory part; good compatible panels are close and cost less. We do not fit a panel we would not put in our own laptop.

Can I get a better screen than the original?

Sometimes. If your model shipped with an HD TN panel, a Full HD IPS panel with the same size and connector often fits. We check the connector, brackets and BIOS support first.

How long does a screen replacement take?

We tell you before you leave the laptop. When the panel is in stock the job is quick; when it has to be ordered for your exact model we give you the expected date rather than a guess.
