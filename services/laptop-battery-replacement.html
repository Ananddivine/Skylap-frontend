Laptop battery replacement in Whitefield

A battery is a consumable: after a few hundred charge cycles it holds less and less. But "not charging" is often the charger or the charging circuit, not the battery. We test all three, then quote.

Quick answer

If the laptop runs fine on the charger but dies quickly or shuts down at 20–40%, the battery is worn and replacement is the fix. If it does not charge at all, we first test the charger, the charging port and the charging IC on the board; a new battery will not fix those. A swollen battery is a safety issue: stop using it and bring it in.

Symptoms this covers

  • Runtime has dropped to under an hour
  • Shuts down suddenly with 20–40% still showing
  • "Plugged in, not charging" or stuck at one percentage
  • Trackpad or keyboard bulging, or the case not sitting flat: a swollen pack
  • Battery not detected, or a red cross on the battery icon
  • Only works with the charger connected

Likely causes, in plain words

CommonWorn cells (normal ageing)
Every lithium pack loses capacity with cycles and heat. Two to four years is typical in Bengaluru temperatures.
CommonCharger or charging port
A frayed cable or a loose jack looks exactly like a bad battery. We test with a known-good charger first. Plugged in, not charging
SometimesCharging IC on the board
The chip that manages charging fails; the battery is fine. A board-level repair, not a battery. Chip-level repair
SometimesBattery IC or a failed cell
One cell or the pack's own controller fails early. Replacement is the practical fix.
RareSoftware or firmware
Battery-care limits, a stuck power driver or old BIOS can cap charging at 60–80%. We check this before selling you anything.

How we diagnose it

  1. We read the battery report: design capacity vs full-charge capacity and cycle count.
  2. We test with a known-good charger and check the port and the charging circuit.
  3. We check for swelling and for heat during charging.
  4. You get a written price for the right pack, with the grade named, before we fit it.

Repair options

Compatible pack

A new pack built for your model. Good compatible cells give near-original runtime at a lower price; we name the grade and the warranty.

Original-grade pack

Where the maker's part is available in India, we offer it and tell you the difference in price and runtime.

Charging repair instead

If the tests show the charger, port or charging IC is the problem, we repair that and you keep your battery.

Repair, or replace the laptop?

Repair makes sense when

  • The laptop is otherwise quick enough for what you do
  • The pack is the only tired part
  • Runtime matters and a new pack restores it

Replacement makes sense when

  • The battery, screen and storage are all worn and the machine is slow
  • The pack is unavailable for a discontinued model
  • You need a lighter or faster machine anyway; we can move your data across

Questions people ask

Before you decide

How do I know if my laptop battery needs replacing?

Runtime under an hour, shutdowns with charge still showing, or a battery report showing full-charge capacity well below design capacity. Swelling is a definite yes, and urgent.

The signs, explained.

Genuine or compatible battery: which do you fit?

Whichever you choose after we explain both. We name the grade on the quote, and the warranty that comes with it. Customers mention "genuine battery" in their reviews because we told them exactly what they were getting.

Battery not charging: is it the battery or the charging IC?

We test in this order: charger, port, charging circuit, battery. A new battery does nothing for a dead charging IC, which is a board-level repair we do in-house.

My battery is swollen. Is it dangerous?

Treat it seriously. Stop charging, do not press on it, and bring the laptop in. Swelling can crack the trackpad and case and, rarely, become a fire risk. We remove and dispose of the pack safely.

Can you replace an internal battery?

Yes. Internal packs sit under the palmrest or base cover and take longer to reach, and a few are glued in. That is part of what decides the price.
