Hinge broken on one side · lid loose · case cracking · screen flickers when opened

Laptop hinge repair

A hinge that has gone stiff, loose or broken on one side gets worse every time you open the lid, and eventually it takes the display cable and the screen with it. Fix it early and it is a small repair.

Quick answer

Two different things break: the hinge itself (metal, wears stiff or snaps) and the plastic mounts it screws into (the lid or base cracks around it). A hinge alone is a quick swap. Torn-out mounts need the cover replaced or the mount rebuilt, which customers call "fabrication". Stop opening the lid wide until it is seen; the cable inside the hinge is the expensive part.

Symptoms this covers

  • Hinge broken on one side; the lid opens unevenly
  • Cracking or a gap at the corners where the lid meets the base
  • Lid very stiff, or so loose it falls back
  • Screen flickers or goes black when you move the lid
  • Plastic pieces falling out when you open it

Likely causes, in plain words

CommonHinge worn stiff
The hinge tension increases with age; force then cracks the plastic mount. Replace the hinge before that happens.
CommonMount torn out of the lid or base
Once the screw posts crack, the hinge floats and grinds the case. The cover is replaced or the mount rebuilt with epoxy and inserts.
SometimesDrop or bag pressure
A single impact cracks the hinge cover; often only the plastics need replacing.
SometimesDisplay cable damaged by the loose hinge
Flicker on lid movement means the cable is already suffering. Fix both together. Flicker and lines

How we diagnose it

  1. We open the hinge cover and see whether the hinge, the mounts or both have failed.
  2. We check the display cable for wear where it passes through the hinge.
  3. We check part availability for your model: hinge pair, back cover, base cover.
  4. One written price, with the choice between a new cover and a rebuilt mount explained.

Repair options

Hinge replacement

A new hinge pair when the mounts are intact. The quickest and cheapest outcome.

Cover replacement

New back cover or base cover when the screw posts have torn out and the part is available for your model.

Mount rebuild ("fabrication")

For models where the cover is unavailable, we rebuild the mount with reinforced inserts. Strong, honest, and we show you the work.

Cable replacement

If the hinge has already worn the display cable, we replace it in the same job.

Repair, or replace the laptop?

Repair makes sense when

  • The laptop is otherwise fine; hinges are a body repair, not a performance one
  • Caught early, before the cable and screen are affected

Replacement makes sense when

  • The body is cracked in several places and the screen and cable are already damaged on an old machine

Questions people ask

Before you decide

The hinge is broken on one side. Can I keep using the laptop?

Use it as little as possible with the lid at one angle. Every open and close grinds the mount further and stresses the display cable. It is a small repair now and a screen repair later.

Can a broken hinge damage the display?

Yes. The display cable runs through the hinge; a loose hinge bends and eventually breaks it, giving flicker, lines or a black screen. That is why we check the cable during every hinge job.

What is hinge "fabrication"?

Rebuilding the plastic mount the hinge screws into when the original cover is unavailable. We use inserts and reinforcement, and you see the result before the laptop is closed up.

Why does a hinge repair sometimes cost more than the hinge?

Because the hinge is rarely the only casualty: cracked covers, torn mounts and worn cables are what take the time and the parts. We list each item on the quote.
