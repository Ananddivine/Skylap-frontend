Lenovo laptop repair in Whitefield

ThinkPads are built to be serviced and worth keeping alive; IdeaPads and Yogas need more care to open. We work on all of them, including the board-level faults that authorised centres answer with a new board.

Get a repair quoteCall SkyLap
SkyLap is an independent workshop, not an authorised Lenovo service centre. That is why we can repair a board at component level instead of replacing it, fit compatible or original parts and tell you which, and work on models the brand no longer supports. Warranty-covered laptops should go to Lenovo first; we say so when that is the better route.

Process

How a repair works here

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Free at the Whitefield bench. Nearby, we collect the laptop from you.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Approve

    Nothing is opened further or ordered until you say yes.

  4. 04

    Repair
