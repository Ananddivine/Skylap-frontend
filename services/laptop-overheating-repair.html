Overheating, noisy fans and heat shutdowns

Dust blocks the fins, thermal paste dries out, and the laptop protects itself by slowing down or switching off. A thermal paste change and deep clean fixes most of it. A fan that rattles or has stopped is replaced.

Quick answer

A laptop that runs hot, gets loud and then slows down is throttling: the processor lowers its speed to stay under its temperature limit. The cause is nearly always dust in the fins and old thermal paste, sometimes a fan that has stopped. Cleaning and re-pasting restores performance; you will notice the difference the same day.

Symptoms this covers

  • Base or keyboard uncomfortably hot
  • Fan running loud constantly, or rattling
  • Games or video calls slow to a crawl after ten minutes
  • Shuts down or restarts under load
  • Fan completely silent while the laptop is hot (fan stopped)

Likely causes, in plain words

CommonDust packed in the heatsink fins
Bengaluru dust builds a felt-like layer between fan and fins. Air stops flowing.
CommonDried thermal paste
After two to three years the paste between chip and heatsink hardens and heat cannot cross. Re-pasting restores it.
SometimesFan failure
Bearings wear (rattle) or the fan stops entirely. Replaced as a unit. Fan noise explained
SometimesBlocked vents from use on beds and sofas
Soft surfaces seal the intake. Free fix: a hard surface or a stand.
RareSoftware keeping the processor busy

How we diagnose it

  1. We log temperatures at idle and under load before touching anything.
  2. We open the laptop, inspect the fan, fins and paste, and check the fan spins freely.
  3. We deep clean, re-paste with a quality compound, and replace the fan if needed.
  4. We log temperatures again and show you both readings.

Repair options

Thermal paste change and deep clean

Full disassembly to the heatsink, dust cleared from fins and fan, fresh paste on CPU and GPU, thermal pads checked.

Fan replacement

A new fan matched to your model when the bearing is gone or the fan has stopped.

Heatsink and fan assembly

On gaming laptops with dual fans and heat pipes, the whole assembly may need replacing if a pipe has failed.

Questions people ask

Before you decide

Is it normal for a laptop fan to run all the time?

Under load, yes. At idle on a desk, no. A fan that never stops usually means the fins are blocked so the fan works harder for less air. Cleaning fixes it.

Can overheating damage my laptop?

Over time, yes: the battery ages faster, solder joints fatigue, and the machine throttles itself constantly. Thermal shutdowns are the laptop protecting itself; they are a warning, not the damage itself.

How often should thermal paste be changed?

Every two to three years for a well-used laptop, sooner for gaming machines run hot every day. If the fan is already loud or the laptop throttles, it is due now.

My gaming laptop throttles after ten minutes. Is that fixable?

Usually. Gaming laptops have the most to gain from a proper clean and re-paste, and customers with an HP Omen or Acer Predator write exactly that in their reviews. If a heat pipe or fan has failed, we replace the assembly.
