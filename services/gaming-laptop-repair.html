Quick answer

Frame drops after ten minutes are heat: dust and dry paste make the CPU and GPU throttle. A thermal service fixes that. A GPU that disappears from the system or shows artefacts is a board-level fault we diagnose on the bench. High-refresh screens and high-wattage chargers are specific parts we source for your exact model.

Symptoms this covers

  • FPS drops and stutter after ten to fifteen minutes
  • Fans at full speed, base too hot to rest on
  • GPU not detected, or artefacts and coloured blocks
  • 144 Hz or 165 Hz screen dead or flickering
  • Battery drains while plugged in during games
  • Random shutdowns mid-game

Likely causes, in plain words

CommonThermal throttling
Dual-fan systems clog quickly. Deep clean and re-paste, with temperatures logged before and after. Thermal service
SometimesGPU or its power stage
The GPU's voltage regulators fail before the chip does; a component repair. Chip reballing is possible but quoted honestly. GPU not detected
SometimesCharger too weak or charging circuit fault
A 65 W charger on a 180 W laptop drains the battery under load. A failed charging IC does the same. Charging repair
SometimesHigh-refresh panel or cable
Gaming panels are model-specific; we identify the exact part. Screen replacement

How we diagnose it

  1. Temperature and clock logging under a real load, so throttling is proven, not guessed.
  2. GPU power-stage and detection tests on the board.
  3. Charger output and charging-circuit checks.
  4. Written finding and price before any part is ordered.

Repair, or replace the laptop?

Repair makes sense when

  • Nearly always: the replacement cost of a gaming laptop is high and the common faults are serviceable
  • The GPU still works when cool

Replacement makes sense when

  • The GPU chip itself is dead with no donor available on an old model

Questions people ask

Before you decide

Can a laptop GPU be repaired or replaced?

The circuits that power the GPU can be repaired at component level, which fixes many "GPU not detected" cases. The GPU chip itself is soldered; replacing it (reballing or a donor chip) is possible on some boards but is a specialist job we quote case by case.

Why does my gaming laptop slow down after ten minutes?

Heat. The CPU and GPU reach their limit and cut their speed. Dust and dry paste are the usual cause; a thermal service brings the speed back.

Do you work on HP Omen, Acer Predator and ASUS ROG?

Yes; these are the three lines customers mention most in their reviews of us. Other gaming brands are welcome too.
