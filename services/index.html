Process

How a repair works here

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Free at the Whitefield bench. Nearby, we collect the laptop from you.

  2. 02

    Quote

    One written price for parts and labour, with what we found and why.

  3. 03

    Approve

    Nothing is opened further or ordered until you say yes.

  4. 04

    Repair

    Component-level where possible; a replacement part only when it is the honest answer.

  5. 05

    Test

    Run and checked before it leaves the bench: power, display, thermals, ports, keyboard.

  6. 06

    Return

    Collect at the workshop, or we drop it back within our pickup areas.
