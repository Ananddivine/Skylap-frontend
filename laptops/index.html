Why buy from a repair workshop

  • Every machine passes the same checks we would run for a customer: battery report, drive health, screen, hinges, ports, thermals.
  • We tell you what was replaced, not just that it was "refurbished".
  • If your current laptop is worth repairing, we will say so instead of selling you a replacement. That is in our reviews more often than any sales line.
  • Upgrades on the spot: more RAM or a bigger SSD fitted before you take it home.

Before you buy any used laptop, anywhere

Run through our refurbished buying checklist. It takes fifteen minutes and it is the difference between a bargain and a repair bill. Ask us to run those checks in front of you.

Not sure whether to buy or repair?

Bring the laptop you have. If a upgrade would fix what annoys you, that is cheaper than a new machine; if it would not, we say that too. The repair-or-replace helper gives you a first read in five questions.
