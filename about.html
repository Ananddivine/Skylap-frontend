01 · The workshop

A bench, not a counter

SkyLap has been on Whitefield Main Road for thirteen years, on the first floor of the building opposite Dress Circle Mall. It is not large. What it has is a proper bench: a power supply, hot air, magnification and the parts drawers that make component-level work possible.

That matters because most laptop "repair" in India is parts replacement. Swapping a screen or a battery needs a screwdriver and the right part. Finding the one failed component on a board that will not power on needs a bench and someone who has done it a few thousand times.

02 · The work

What we actually do differently

When a laptop will not turn on, the authorised route is to replace the motherboard, because that is the process those centres are built around. We measure the board instead: the input stage, then the standby rails, then the main rails, until the first missing voltage points at the area, then find the failed part under magnification and replace it.

The result is that the laptop keeps its own board, its own storage and its own data, at a fraction of a board price. Twenty-five of our reviewers describe arriving after another centre said "not repairable" or quoted a replacement.

04 · The technician

Ask for Amal

Thirty-five of our reviews name a person, and Amal appears most often. He runs the bench and does the board-level work himself: power diagnostics, charging circuits, IC replacement and the microsoldering most shops send away.

When something needs opening up, he is the one who explains what happened and what it will cost. Customers mention it because it is unusual, not because we ask them to.

05 · Honest limits

What we do not claim

  • We are an independent workshop, not an authorised service centre for any brand. In-warranty laptops should go to the brand first, and we say so.
  • We do not promise a turnaround before we have seen the laptop. Many jobs are same-day; board work and ordered parts are not.
  • We do not publish a price for a board fault before diagnosing it, because that number would be a guess.
  • When a repair is not worth your money, we tell you.
