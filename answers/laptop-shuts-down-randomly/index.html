Laptop keeps shutting down randomly

A laptop that turns itself off is protecting itself or losing power. The pattern tells you which: off under load points at heat, off at a fixed battery percentage points at the battery, off at random with a restart points at software or the power section.

Updated 2026-09-10Written from the Whitefield bench, checked against manufacturer guidance

Quick answer

Shuts down when hot or busy: thermal protection, fix the cooling. Shuts down at 20–40% battery but fine on the charger: worn battery. Restarts with a blue screen: software or a driver. Cuts out completely at random on the charger too: a power-section or charging fault on the board. Note when it happens and you have half the diagnosis.

Likely causes

CommonThermal shutdown
Under load, after minutes, base hot: the processor hit its limit. Clean and re-paste. Overheating explained
CommonWorn battery
The pack reports 30% but cannot deliver current, so the laptop drops. Fine on the charger. Battery replacement
SometimesSoftware or driver crash
Blue screen then restart. Often a driver, a failing update or bad RAM. Windows repair
SometimesPower section or charging fault
Cuts out on the charger with no warning and no blue screen. Board-level. Chip-level repair
RareLoose battery or power connector
A connector that has worked loose after a drop. Quick to fix.

Safe checks you can do first

  1. Note the pattern for a day: under load, at a battery level, with a blue screen, or at random on the charger.
  2. Feel the base when it happens. Hot means thermal.
  3. Run on the charger only for a day. If the shutdowns stop, it is the battery.
  4. Check Windows Event Viewer for "Kernel-Power" or bug-check entries after a shutdown; a blue screen code points at software.
  5. Run the built-in memory diagnostic if you see blue screens.

What not to do

  • Do not ignore thermal shutdowns; each one is heat the board and battery did not need.
  • Do not reinstall Windows for a laptop that shuts down when hot; it will not help.
  • Do not run a swollen or very hot battery; remove it or bring the laptop in.

When it is a repair, and which one

Thermal: a cleaning and re-paste. Battery: replacement after we confirm it with a battery report and a load test. Software: a Windows repair or driver fix. Power section: component repair on the board. We reproduce the shutdown at the bench before quoting so the fix matches the cause.

Questions people ask

Before you decide

Why does my laptop turn off by itself when I play games?

Heat. Games load everything at once and a blocked cooling path cannot keep up. A thermal service fixes this; a gaming laptop with dual fans benefits most.

Why does my laptop shut down at 30% battery?

The battery is worn: it reports charge it cannot deliver. A new pack fixes it. Confirm with a battery report first.

My laptop restarts with a blue screen. Is that hardware?

Usually software or a driver, sometimes RAM. The code on the blue screen matters; note it or photograph it.
