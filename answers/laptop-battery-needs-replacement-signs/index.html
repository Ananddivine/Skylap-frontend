How to know if your laptop battery needs replacing

Batteries wear out gradually, then suddenly. The signs are consistent across HP, Dell, Lenovo, ASUS and Acer, and Windows can measure the wear for you in a couple of minutes.

Updated 2026-09-10Written from the Whitefield bench, checked against manufacturer guidance

Quick answer

Replace the battery when runtime has fallen to a fraction of what it was, when the laptop shuts down with 20–40% still showing, or when the battery report shows full-charge capacity far below design capacity. Replace it urgently if the case, trackpad or keyboard is bulging: that is a swollen pack. If the laptop simply will not charge, test the charger and port first; that is a different fault.

Likely causes

CommonNormal cycle wear
Every lithium pack loses capacity over a few hundred cycles; heat speeds it up. Two to four years is typical.
SometimesA failed cell in the pack
Sudden collapse of runtime and shutdowns at a fixed percentage.
SometimesSwelling
Gas builds inside ageing cells. Visible as a bulge. Stop using it.
SometimesNot the battery: charger, port or charging IC
A laptop that will not charge at all usually has a charging fault, not a worn pack. Plugged in, not charging

Safe checks you can do first

  1. Windows: open Command Prompt and run powercfg /batteryreport; open the saved report and compare Design Capacity with Full Charge Capacity. Below about 60–70% of design is worn.
  2. Check the cycle count in the same report; several hundred cycles with poor runtime confirms wear.
  3. Look at the laptop on a flat surface: any rocking, a lifted trackpad or a bulging base means swelling.
  4. Time the runtime doing normal work; compare with what it was when new.

What not to do

  • Do not press on a swollen battery or keep charging it.
  • Do not "calibrate" by draining to zero repeatedly; it accelerates wear.
  • Do not buy a battery for a laptop that will not charge at all before the charger is tested.

Questions people ask

Before you decide

How do I know if my Dell / HP laptop battery needs replacing?

Same test on both: the Windows battery report, plus the runtime and shutdown signs above. Dell and HP diagnostic tools also grade the battery health and say "replace" when it is time.

How long does a laptop battery last before it needs replacing?

Typically two to four years of daily use in Bengaluru temperatures; less if the laptop runs hot or is kept plugged in at 100% all day.

Windows says "battery not detected". Is it dead?

Sometimes the connector, the battery's own controller, or a driver. Sometimes a dead pack. We test before selling you one.
